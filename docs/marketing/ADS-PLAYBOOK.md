# Digi ads playbook (from the Origins course + our research)

**Course:** Origins (skool.com/origins), bought **Nov 17, 2026**, $98 a month. **Cancel or renew decision: about Dec 15**, before it renews.

**Goal:** learn the e-commerce ad mindset (creative testing, hooks, scaling) and apply it to Digi first, then Kilma and the game.

## Course priority (skip the rest)
1. Ads and creative modules: hooks, angles, UGC, editing, testing.
2. Copywriting program, released weekly.
3. Metrics: when to kill or scale, budgets, ROAS.
4. Group calls: bring the questions below.
5. The 2 ad reviews a month: use them on **real Digi ad videos**.

**Skip:** product research, store setup, suppliers.

## Questions to ask the coaches
1. For an **app** (installs and trial starts, not purchases), what changes in their testing framework?
2. How many creatives per test, what budget per creative, and how long before killing one?
3. Best hook formats for a visual before/after product (a normal photo turning into a digicam flash shot)?
4. UGC creators: where they find them, what they pay, how they brief them.
5. How do they turn winning organic TikToks into ads?
6. Broad targeting vs interests in 2026?
7. Which numbers they read first: hook rate, hold rate, CTR, CPM, cost per result.
8. Scaling: raise the budget on the same ad set vs duplicate it, and how fast.
9. How do they spot creative fatigue and refresh?
10. What would they test first for Digi's launch week with $300–500?

## App-specific notes (what differs from e-commerce)
- **Optimise for app installs, then trial start / purchase events** through RevenueCat and Meta's tracking.
- **iOS 14+ privacy limits what Meta can track.** Expect fewer conversions reported than real ones. Judge by RevenueCat revenue per install (D7 cash ÷ installs).
- **The App Store page converts too:** screenshots, first frame, Custom Product Pages per ad angle (ch. 12).
- **Break-even cost per install** must come from Digi's real numbers (the ch. 26 method). Never scale above it.
