## What the owner clearly likes
- **The pixel logo.** "isn't the pixelated logo better": yes, 8.5/10, because it's real digicam language and survives tiny sizes as a watermark. It should be prominent (his concept puts DIGI big at the top), not only on welcome.
- **The camera as the app:** a digicam body with a big viewfinder, a REC/date overlay, battery, and FLASH/3.2M HUD.
- **The combo's Camera and Looks:** big viewfinder, text mode tabs (Y2K · FILM · DIGICAM · STRIP · XMAS), the "Tonight's roll · 18 left" pill, and the shutter centred. **No** looks-bubble row and **no** extra clutter.
- Pink body, dark UI, Instrument Serif italic pink headline words, orange LCD date stamp.
