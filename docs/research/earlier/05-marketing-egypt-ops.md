**Research slice 05. Checked Nov 16, 2026.** This is research, not legal or tax advice. Anything about tax, banking or platform rules goes to a US CPA (cross-border), an Egyptian accountant, or the platform's own support before he acts on it.

**Labels:** **V** = verified today against the source linked. **C** = claimed by a source I couldn't check against a primary document (vendor blogs, seller statements, third-party lists). **E** = my estimate or inference.

## 0. The blunt version (read this if nothing else)

1. **The contest will be won or lost on organic TikTok/Instagram slideshows plus share links. Not ads.** The app goes live around Nov 21–24, so it has about 3 weeks to sell. Every proven app in this niche grew on carousels, not paid ads: Boothie ($54.4K MRR, "no paid ads") and POV Disposable ($200K MRR from a dozen niche slideshow accounts) [V/C, §1].
2. **Paid ads lose money inside a 30-day profit window.** Apple Ads Photo & Video median CPI in the US is $2.79 [V]. A payer is worth roughly $18 net inside 30 days [E]. Break-even needs about 15% of installs to pay, and the median download-to-paid for AI apps is 2.4% [V]. Cap any test at **$250 total** and only boost posts that are already winning (§3).
3. **The Egypt geography problem is real, and the popular fix breaks the rules.** The growth-hacker playbook is a VPN, GPS off, a US phone and bought "warmed" US accounts [C, Nevo/Postiz]. Buying accounts breaks TikTok ToS §3.2 ("Do not ... transfer your account") [V]. Faking location goes against §3.2's accuracy promise and the "inauthentic behaviour" rules [V], and it risks bans that also wreck the exit story. **Clean route:** (a) US creators post from their own accounts with paid-partnership labels, and we boost them with Spark Ads; (b) a US-based part-time posting assistant runs a brand account, with access granted through TikTok Business Center; (c) lean harder on Instagram, Pinterest and YouTube Shorts, where search drives more of the reach (§2).
4. **ASO has a real gap.** The "photo strip" and "photo booth" results in the US App Store are full of apps with 0–100 ratings. "Digicam" is crowded (Dazz Cam has 118K ratings). Apple's own search hints show "photo strips", "photo booth app for events", "digital camera filter" and "photo dump" [V]. A new competitor, **"stripz: group photo strips"** (June 2026, 34 ratings), already owns the "group strips" phrase [V]. Our differentiation has to be the link-based group strip plus the digicam/flash looks, not "group strips" alone.
5. **Banking from Egypt works, with caveats.** Egypt is *not* on Mercury's 48-country prohibited list (third-party copy, Sep/Oct 2026) [C]. Mercury does require "existing or planned US operations" and an account can be closed without them [C, Mercury support via Foundlie/bizstartz]. Wise doesn't list Egypt as restricted [V]. Relay needs US physical addresses, so it's out [C]. **Open Mercury now, keep Wise Business as a backup, and use Payoneer only for EGP cash-out if needed.**
6. **US paperwork is light but has one trap.** Form 5472 plus a pro-forma 1120 is due April 15, 2027 for 2026, with a **$25,000 penalty** for missing it [V, multiple CPA sources]. BOI/CTA reporting no longer applies to US-formed LLCs, even foreign-owned ones; FinCEN's final rule took effect Aug 14, 2026 [V, law-firm alerts]. "No US income tax" is *probably* right if there's no US trade or business and no US permanent establishment (US–Egypt treaty), but hiring US contractors could change that. **Ask a CPA [E].**

**What this means for Digi [E]:**
- The *format* is settled: 4–7 slide carousels with a short hook, the reveal on slide 2–3, the app UI on one slide, and a trending sound.
- The *volume* is what matters: 2–3 posts a day per account, winners re-cut 5–10 times.
- **Trends die fast.** Lensa's category lost about three-quarters of its spend in 8 weeks. Christmas, then NYE, is the window, so front-load everything.
- Invite gates like Lapse's spike and crash, and they read as dark patterns. Our share link must be a *gift* (the friend gets the strip) and never a forced gate.
- Remini's lesson: when a post goes viral, conversion is the bottleneck. The paywall right after the first result and the share watermark must be ready on day 1.

### 1.2 Ground rules for every post (lawyer guidance + platform rules)
- **OK:** a faceless brand account ("Digi"), and a brand persona that is clearly the brand.
- **NOT OK:**
  - fake reviews or testimonials;
  - AI "people" posing as real customers ("I tried this app and…");
  - fake "friends reacting" videos;
  - multiple accounts to farm free credits.
- The FTC's rule on consumer reviews and testimonials (in force since Oct 21, 2024) explicitly covers AI-generated fake testimonials, with civil penalties around $51.7K per violation [V, [FTC](https://www.ftc.gov/news-events/news/press-releases/2024/08/federal-trade-commission-announces-final-rule-banning-fake-reviews-testimonials)].
- **Label AI images.** TikTok "require[s] creators to label AI-generated or significantly edited content that shows realistic-looking scenes or people"; unlabeled content "may be removed, restricted, or labeled" [V, [TikTok Integrity & Authenticity, effective Sep 24, 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/community-guidelines/en/integrity-authenticity)]. Turn on the AI-generated label on every before/after that uses AI-made fictional people.
- **Multiple accounts are allowed, but not to deceive.** TikTok says "You can have multiple accounts... but not to deceive others" [V, same page]. Three openly branded accounts (e.g. @digi.pink, @digi.strips, @digi.xmas) are fine. A "persona" that pretends to be a random US college girl who discovered an app is not.
- **Real faces** only from the owner, or from friends who gave written consent (this is also a contest rule). Otherwise use clearly AI-made fictional people with the AI label.
- **Paid creators** must switch on TikTok's branded content / "Paid partnership" toggle (required by TikTok ToS §3.7, which cites the FTC Endorsement Guides [V, [ToS Jul 15, 2026](https://www.tiktok.com/legal/page/us/terms-of-service/en)]) and add #ad in the caption [E: safest practice].

## 2. The TikTok geography problem and the clean US-reach plan

### 2.1 How the problem works
- **TikTok sets an account's country from where its user seems to be.** Signals include SIM region, IP, device settings, language, time zone and activity, and the account region is set at sign-up [C, secondary summaries of TikTok help: [PCrisk](https://www.pcrisk.com/blog/tips/14036-how-to-change-tiktok-location), [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/how-to-change-tiktok-account-region/)].
- **The growth crowd assumes posts get shown to that country first.** Their own guide says "TikTok distribution depends on the account's current location" [C, Nevo/Postiz].
- **So a new account made in Cairo will likely be tested on an Egyptian/MENA audience first [E].** Those viewers rarely pay $7.99 a week in USD. The contest only counts money.

### 2.2 What the rules actually say (cited)

| Practice | TikTok rule | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| VPN / "location spoofing" | ToS (Jul 15, 2026) doesn't mention VPNs [V]. §3.2: info you give "is and will remain complete and accurate" [V]. The Community Guidelines ban accounts that "mislead or try to manipulate our platform" [V]; an older version (quoted in a California AG filing) lists misleading people about an account's "approximate location" as part of covert influence [C] | **Grey and risky.** Not an explicit ban, but it fits "misleading"; VPN users report 0-view shadowbans [C]. **Don't build the contest on it.** |
| Buying "warmed" US accounts | ToS §3.2: "Do not give others access to your account, or transfer your account to anyone else, without our permission" [V] | **Breaks the ToS. No.** It's also a liability at exit, since a buyer's due diligence would find it [E]. |
| Several brand accounts | "You can have multiple accounts... but not to deceive others" [V] | **OK** if each one is openly Digi |
| Persona accounts pretending to be real customers | ToS §3.4 "inauthentic commercial behaviors"; the guidelines ban impersonation and fake reviews [V]; FTC fake-review rule [V] | **No** |
| Team members posting on a brand account | TikTok Business Center lets an admin give members access to a TikTok account, including posting and managing it [C, [TikTok Ads help](https://ads.tiktok.com/help/article/how-to-grant-members-access-to-tiktok-accounts-in-business-center)] | **OK.** This is TikTok's own "permission" route under §3.2 |
| Paid creators posting about Digi | Allowed with the branded-content toggle (ToS §3.7) [V] | **OK** |
| Spark Ads on creator posts aimed at the US | Creator makes a code per post (7/30/60/365 days); advertiser enters it in Ads Manager [C, [Insense guide](https://insense.pro/blog/tiktok-spark-ads)]. - Apple Small Business Program commission: 15% (RevenueCat counts revenue; the contest subtracts fees).
- Payer mix: 70% weekly, 30% annual.
- Weekly payer inside 30 days ≈ 1 + 0.45 + 0.45×0.65 + 0.45×0.65×0.75 ≈ 1.96 payments → $15.66 gross → **$13.31 net**.
- Annual payer → **$33.99 net**.
- Blended ≈ **$19.5 net per payer**, minus AI cost (~$1.50 per payer per month) ≈ **$18 per payer in-window**.
- Refunds: −4% → about **$17.3**.

**Break-even:** CPI ÷ value per payer = required install-to-paid rate.

| Channel | CPI assumed | Break-even install→paid in 30 days | Realistic install→paid [E] | Result |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Apple Ads, exact match on long-tail ("photo strip", "photo booth app for events", brand "digi photo booth") | $2.79 (median; long-tail may be ~$1.5) | 16% (9% at $1.5) | 5–10% (high-intent search + hard paywall after first result) | **−$1.0 to −$2.0 per install**; break-even only at the low CPT plus a strong paywall |
| TikTok Spark Ads (US/UK/AU) | ~$3–4 | 17–23% | 2–5% | **−$2.5 to −$3.5 per install** |
| Meta app ads | cost per payer ≈ $43–65 | needs $43–65 per payer vs $17 | — | **−$26 to −$48 per payer** |

**Verdict:** inside a 30-day profit contest, **paid is a cost, not a growth engine.** Run one tiny, capped experiment, mainly to learn real numbers for after the contest. Annual renewals and resale value aren't counted in the contest, but they matter for the exit.

**The test (hard cap $250, everything in RevenueCat + Apple Ads dashboards):**
1. **Apple Ads Advanced, US only, from launch day to Dec 7:** $10/day.
   - Exact match: "photo strips", "photo strip maker", "photo booth app", "photo booth app for events", "christmas photo booth", "digicam photo booth", plus the brand "digi photo".
   - Max CPT bid $1.20.
   - **Kill rule:** cost per paying user > $17 after 40 taps.
   - Never bid on competitor names (Boothie, WayShot). It's legal on Apple Ads, but it invites retaliation and looks bad in a listing [E].
   - Budget: about $120.
2. **Spark Ads:** only after an organic or creator post beats 3× the account median. $25/day for 4 days, US/UK/AU, women 16–34, optimise for app installs. About $100.
   - **Kill rule:** cost per install > $3 after day 2.
