## Key takeaways

1. **Say "AI" out loud, every time.** The angriest recent reviews of digicam-style AI apps are about hidden AI, not price. WayShot reviews: "Maybe mention your app is an AI photo editor?" and "they never disclose this is generative AI based" [V, US review RSS]. "AI " appears in **9.8% of recent 1–2★ reviews** across 15 camera and AI photo apps, against 2.1% of 4–5★ ones [V, 2,848 reviews]. Mark every AI look with ✦ and the word "AI".
2. **"Free" is the most double-edged word in this category.** It appears in **17.3% of 1–2★ reviews** and 5.4% of 4–5★ ones [V]. Happy users say it about the camera ("it's literally a free app", Dazz). Angry users say it about trials ("Free app but isnt free", WayShot). Rule: use "free" only for things that are free forever. For the trial, the price sits next to "free", the same size.
3. **Money words cluster in angry reviews.** "Scam" is in 11.9% of 1–2★ reviews and 0.2% of 4–5★. "Subscription" 13.7% vs 0.7%. "Trial" 11.6% vs 0.7%. "Refund" 7.3% vs 0% [V]. Digi's money screens should read like a receipt: what you get, the price, the date you pay, how to cancel.
4. **Apple's rules now name AI directly.** Guideline 5.1.2(i), current version dated Oct 16, 2026, says you "must clearly disclose where personal data will be shared with third parties, including with third-party AI, and obtain explicit permission before doing so" [V]. The consent screen in earlier chapters did not name the AI partner. Fixed in section 8.1.
5. **Plain beats slang in brand copy.** A dcdx poll found **85% of 92 Gen Z respondents** called brands using "bestie" cringe [C; the poll is from Aug 2024, re-reported by Confectionery News on Aug 21, 2026]. A *Journal of Marketing Research* study (Pyrah et al., Oct 2025) found brand slang tends to read as inauthentic, hurting "sincere" brands more than "exciting" ones, and the penalty disappears when an influencer posts it [C]. Let creators and users bring the slang; Digi's UI stays short and plain with a little wit.
6. **The users' own words are better than ours.** Real review phrases: "literal digicam vibes", "the yellow date thing", "makes taking pics fun again", "without the price" of a real camera, and on Lapse, "being able to make 'roll's' together" [V]. Use these in store copy and TikTok captions.
7. **Most apps use "Start free trial" and "Cancel anytime"; that's fine.** Adapty's State of Paywalls 2026 (78,000+ paywalls; the button and note counts come from its sample of the **100 top iOS paywalls**): 36% of buttons say "Start free trial" word for word, and of the 61% of trial paywalls with a cancel note, 68% say "Cancel anytime" [C, Adapty, Nov 1, 2026]. Visual or copy tests raised LTV in only **35%** of tests [C]. Don't spend contest traffic on copy A/B tests. Use the three paywall variants for three different *moments* instead.
8. **Arabic: ads, weekly billing and "no Arabic" are what annoy Gulf and Egyptian users.** In 175 recent Arabic reviews from the Egypt and Saudi stores, ads were the top complaint (31 mentions, 26 of them in 1–2★ reviews). Others asked why billing is weekly instead of monthly or yearly, or called the app a failure for having no Arabic [V, small sample]. Egyptian praise words: "خطير", "تحفة", "جامد", "حلو أوي". Saudi: "يجنن", "يهبل", "روعة" [V].
9. **Recommended voice in one line:** *"A friend with a good camera who tells you the price before you ask."* Short sentences, nouns over adjectives, one emoji per screen at most, and every number on screen.

**Patterns worth copying [E, high]:**
- **Short declarative sentences, two at most, before any list.** Hipstamatic, Sparkle, Party Cam.
- **Name the moment, not the feature.** WayShot's look guide is the best copy in the niche: "Going out? Start with DigiCam. Shooting in daylight? Try DigiLite." [V].
- **Steps beat adjectives for group apps.** Every event app (POV, Once, Scene, Party Cam, AllShots) explains the loop in 3–4 numbered steps, and every one says some version of "No download required for guests" [V].
- **Honest privacy lines are now standard in AI apps.** Sparkle: "Your photos are private to your account, never shown to anyone else, and never used to train AI. Delete your account any time in Profile and everything goes with it." [V].
- **Trademark disclaimers.** Sparkle: "G7X is a trademark of its owner. Sparkle is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by any camera maker." Huji opens with a capital-letters Fujifilm disclaimer [V]. Digi should not use camera brand names at all (section 9).

### 3.4 Convert vs scam: the rules [E, high, built on 3.1–3.3]

| Feels honest, converts | Feels like a scam |
|---|---|
| A number: "60 AI digis a month" | "Unlimited" (with fair use hidden) |
| A date: "You won't be charged until Nov 27" | "Free trial" with the price in grey 11 pt |
| "AI" stated before the result | AI revealed after the face changes |
| "Your digi's back" when a job fails | Silent credit loss |
| One paywall view in onboarding, ✕ at once | "Try for free" on every launch |
| "The camera's free. Always." (true) | "Free app" on a hard-paywalled app |
| Same price everywhere: store, paywall, push | A launch price that quietly disappears |
| The user's own photo as the hero | Ad images better than real results |

## 4. Gen Z voice, late 2026: what is current and what is cringe

### 4.1 What the evidence says

- **Brand slang usually backfires.** "More often than not, the use of slang backfires on the brand and they lose credibility" (Bryce Pyrah, co-author of a 2025 *Journal of Marketing Research* study). The penalty disappeared when an influencer used the same slang [C, Confectionery News, Aug 21, 2026].
- **"Bestie": 85% of 92 Gen Z respondents called brands using it cringe** (dcdx poll, Aug 2024) [C, same source; small, two-year-old poll].
- **Retired or dated (Jan 2026 guide):** cheugy, "it's giving", slay, bussin, "tell me you're X without telling me", vibe check. **Risky:** no cap, rizz, Ohio, unc, skibidi, 6-7, sigma, mewing [C, Colorado State University social media guide, Jan 20, 2026].
- **Still usable, lightly:** lowkey/highkey, POV, core memory, side quest, canon / not canon, "-core", main character, delulu (about yourself only) [C, same guide].
- **The mood behind the trend is anti-polish.** NPR (Jul 18, 2026): people are "unphoning or dephoning their lives". Sunstar notes creators post "digicam dumps" [C]. So words like "perfect", "flawless", "HD" and "studio" fight the trend.

### 4.2 Digi's slang policy [E, high]

| Zone | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|
| **UI (buttons, errors, money)** | No slang. Plain words, contractions OK. | "Save", "Try another look", "You weren't charged." |
| **Light moments (toasts, empty states, success)** | One small wink per screen, max. | "Darkroom's packed." "That's a digi." |
| **Push (social/transactional)** | Plain, with names and numbers. | "Sara's NYE developed 📸" |
| **TikTok / IG captions** | Users' own words; light current terms. | "digicam dump", "core memory", "POV:", "lowkey", "the yellow date thing" |
| **Never anywhere** | Retired terms or ones that read as a brand trying to fit in. | bestie, slay, it's giving, no cap, rizz, bussin, vibe check, main character energy, era (as in "your flash era") |

**Two tests before any line ships [E, high]:**
1. Would a 24-year-old text this to a friend? If not, cut it.
2. Would it embarrass you in a screenshot on X next to a 1★ review? If yes, cut it.

## 5. Trial and pricing wording that passes App Review

### 5.1 The rules, quoted [V, Apple App Review Guidelines, Oct 16, 2026]

| Guideline | Exact text (short) | What it means for Digi's copy |
|---|---|---|
| 3.1.2(c) | "Before asking a customer to subscribe, you should clearly describe what the user will get for the price. How many issues per month?" | The paywall must say "60 AI digis every month", not "more digis" |
| 3.1.2 | "Apps that attempt to scam users will be removed… This includes apps that attempt to trick users into purchasing a subscription under false pretenses or engage in bait-and-switch" | No price that changes after the CTA; no fake trial toggle |
| 3.1.2 | Subscribers should "get what they've paid for without performing additional tasks, such as posting on social media" | Never "Share to unlock" for paying users |
| 2.3.1(a) | "promoting a false price, whether within or outside of the App Store, is grounds for removal" | Launch-week price must be real and have a real end date |
| 2.3.7 | "Metadata such as app names, subtitles, screenshots, and previews should not include prices, terms…"; subtitles must not "reference other apps, or make unverifiable product claims" | No "Free" or "$" in name/subtitle; no "#1"; no "like Dazz" |
| 4.5.4 | Promo pushes only after customers "have explicitly opted in… via consent language displayed in your app's UI" plus an in-app opt-out | Separate toggle and consent line for offers |
| 5.1.2(i) | "clearly disclose where personal data will be shared with third parties, including with third-party AI, and obtain explicit permission" | Name the AI partner at the consent step |
| 5.6 | Apps must not "trick them into making unwanted purchases… raise prices in a tricky manner" | No pre-ticked add-ons, no struck "original" prices that never existed |
| 5.6.1 | "we will disallow custom review prompts" | Use the system rating API only; no "Love Digi? Rate 5★" pre-screen that filters unhappy users |

### 5.2 Safe vs unsafe phrasings

| Situation | Safe ✅ | Unsafe ❌ | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trial CTA | "Start my 3 free days" + line "Then $39.99/year. You won't be charged until Nov 27." | "Try for free" alone | Price and date missing (3.1.2(c), 5.6) |
| Trial card | "3 days free, then $39.99/year" | "FREE" in big type, "$39.99/yr" in small grey | Mismatched prominence: the top complaint in 3.2 |
| Weekly | "$6.99 / week · Cancel anytime" | "Just $0.99/day!" | A per-day price on a weekly SKU hides the billed amount |
| Yearly per-week line | "$0.77 a week, billed $39.99 yearly" (small, under the real price) | "$0.77/week" as the biggest number | Billed amount must be the biggest |
| Savings badge | "Best value" | "Save 89%" (true vs weekly, but reads as a trick); "~~$79.99~~ $39.99" if $79.99 was never charged | 5.6 "raise prices in a tricky manner"; fake anchors |
| Allowance | "60 AI digis every month" | "Unlimited*" | Pecra's 1★ reviews |
| Urgency | "Launch week price · ends Dec 1" | "Only 2 hours left!", a timer that resets | 2.3.1 false price; fake countdown |
| Close | ✕ visible from frame 1 | ✕ after 5 s, "No thanks, I hate fun" | 5.6; guilt copy |
| Restore | "Restore purchases" on every paywall | Hidden in Settings only | Reviewers test it (E, high) |
| Packs | "25 AI digis. Pay once. They don't expire." | "25 digis" with no word on expiry | Expiry is what pack buyers ask first (E, med) |
| AI | "AI look ✦ · uses 1 digi" | "Filter" for an AI result | WayShot reviews (3.2) |

Renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions. [Terms](https://digi.pink/terms) · [Privacy](https://digi.pink/privacy)

## 6. Arabic and Egyptian copy: what the data says

### 6.1 Evidence [V unless marked]

- **Arabic search demand for this niche is close to zero** (chapter 12: no Apple autocomplete for كاميرا قديمة, كاميرا فيلم, فوتو بوث in the UAE and Saudi stores). Arabic copy is for *conversion and trust*, not search.
- **But a missing Arabic UI costs trust in the Gulf.** Saudi reviews: "لاتوجد لغة عربية فاشل" ("no Arabic, a failure", Remini 1★); "نأمل يكون هناك لغة عربية شاملة في البرنامج" ("we hope for full Arabic in the app", Locket 5★). 7 of 175 Arabic reviews mention Arabic.
- **Ads are the top complaint** (31 mentions, 26 of them in 1–2★ reviews). One Saudi reviewer wrote that Locket's ads "مخلة بالعقيدة والفطرة" ("offend faith and instinct"). Digi has no ads. Say so: "من غير إعلانات".
- **Weekly billing confuses people.** "ليه كل اسبوع؟ ابقه شهر او سنه مافي؟" ("why every week? isn't there a month or a year?", Remini 1★, Mecca). This supports chapter 06's test T3: a monthly option in the Middle East and North Africa.
- **Scam words in Arabic:** "نصب" (scam/con), "محتالون" (fraudsters), "سحبو فلوسها" ("they took her money"). Avoid any copy that sounds like it hides a charge.
- **Praise words.** Egypt: "خطيرررر", "تحفه اوي اوي", "جامد جدا", "حلو اوييي", "جميل اوي". Saudi: "يجنن", "يهبل", "روعة", "مرة" (very).

### 6.2 Rules for Digi's Arabic [E, med-high]

1. **Two registers.**
   - **In-app UI: "white" Arabic.** Short, simple Modern Standard Arabic with everyday words any Egyptian or Gulf user reads without friction ("صور", "حفلة", "تجربة مجانية").
   - **Egypt TikTok and invite messages: Egyptian dialect** ("ببلاش", "يلا", "بكرة الصبح", "اتحمّضت").
   - Reason: the Gulf pays more (Boothie's Saudi weekly price is 133% of its US price, chapter 06), and a fully Egyptian UI reads as local and informal to Saudi users.
2. **Gendered imperatives.** Arabic commands are male or female ("اضغط" / "اضغطي"). Use nouns and statements where you can ("التصوير جاهز" instead of "صوّر"). Where a command is unavoidable, the masculine singular is the standard default in Egyptian apps. The women-focused TikTok account can use feminine forms ("جرّبي").
3. **Keep the brand words in Latin letters:** Digi, Plus, AI, Roll. Write "الـAI" with the Arabic article. Plural of digi: "ديجي" after numbers ("٢٥ ديجي"), matching how Arabic counts loan words (E, med).
4. **Numbers:** let iOS format them from the device locale (`Intl.NumberFormat`). Don't hard-code Arabic-Indic or Western digits. The date stamp *on the photo* stays Latin digits (`11 17 '26`), because it is part of the look.
5. **Cultural fit for Egypt and the Gulf:**
   - Name the NYE look "Glitter Countdown", not "Champagne".
   - No drinks in sample images.
   - Occasions that fit: سهرة (night out), خروجة (outing), عيد ميلاد (birthday), خطوبة (engagement), فرح (wedding), راس السنة (New Year's Eve).
   - Christmas matters less in Egypt and the Gulf than NYE, weddings, Eid and graduations (E, med).
6. **Arrows flip in RTL.** "→" becomes "←". Use logical layout (start/end), not left/right.

## What this means for Digi

1. **Fix the consent screen this week (before submission).** Replace the A6b privacy line with: "To make it, we send this one photo to our AI partner, {partner}. It's deleted from their servers and ours within {24 hours}. Not used to train AI." Ticking the box = explicit permission (5.1.2(i)). Confirm the provider name and both retention times in the backend first; put the same sentence in the privacy policy.
2. **Put the word "AI" and ✦ on every AI surface:** look tiles ("AI look ✦"), the shutter counter, the result screen, AI share cards (`AI look ✦ · digi.pink`), and the description's "AI LOOKS ✦" section. This removes the #1 non-money complaint in the niche.
3. **Ship paywall Variant A as written (launch → Dec 12),** with the CTA "Start my 3 free days" and the dated line "You won't be charged until {date}." Wire Variant B to *out of digis* and Friday/Saturday evenings. Switch to Variant C on **Dec 13** in RevenueCat. Don't A/B test copy during the contest.

7. **Lock the date stamp as `11 17 '26` plus `digi.pink`** on every free image from version 1.0. Never add a watermark later to people who didn't have one (Locket backlash, Nov 15, 2026). Say "small digi.pink mark" on the paywall and in the description.

10. **For TikTok and creators,** hand over the caption templates in 8.3 and the users' own phrases ("the yellow date thing", "literal digicam vibes", "makes taking pics fun again"). Brand accounts stay plain; slang belongs to creators.
