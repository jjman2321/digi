## Key takeaways

1. **Instagram is where Digi's buyers are. Snapchat is where the party happens.**
   - Instagram: **79% of US adults aged 18–29** use it, and 56% of US women [V, Pew, survey Jan 30–Jun 18, 2026].
   - Snapchat: 56% of 18–29s use it, and 30% of women.
   - TikTok: 65% of 18–29s.
   - For a camera aimed at women, Instagram reaches more of them than TikTok does.
2. **Instagram's ranking rewards "sends".** Mosseri: "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends… sends are slightly more important for unconnected content" [V, Social Media Today, Jan 22, 2025]. Content that reaches non-followers is content people DM to a friend. So every Digi post should give the viewer a reason to send it: "send this to whoever's hosting NYE."
3. **Sharing to an Instagram Story can't carry a link. Sharing to Snapchat can.**
   - Instagram's Stories API takes a background image and a sticker image only, and has needed a Facebook App ID since January 2023 [V, Meta docs].
   - Snapchat's Creative Kit can attach a URL to the Snap [V, Snap docs], **but only for "select developers and for pre-approved use cases"** (news, music, long-form video, donations, polls, RSVPs), and links that are "direct app downloads" are banned [V, Snap review page, re-checked Nov 17]. Digi must ask for it in the review and may be refused. Every Snap Kit integration needs Snap's app review, which "typically" takes **10–15 business days** [V].
   - **So apply to Snap Kit this week.** On Instagram, the link has to live *inside the image*, in the date-stamp watermark.
4. **Cash paid to influencers is unlikely to pay back inside the contest.**
   - The average amount brands actually paid in 2025 was $193 per Instagram post, $186 per TikTok and $154 per UGC piece [C, Collabstr 2026 report via inBeat].
   - Median micro-creator fee: $150 [C, OpenSponsorship Jan 2025–Jun 2026].
   - A $150 post needs about **9 paying users inside the window** to break even, which takes roughly **40K–75K views** of that one post (E, low–med).
   - **Better ways to spend:** buy *assets with rights* (about $99 per UGC video at Billo, rights included [C, Billo, Nov 10 2026]), and pay creators on **rev-share**, which can never lose money.
5. **The contest rules make one creator mistake fatal.** "Having … anyone you pay buy it" counts as cheating [V, rules quoted in 09].
   - A paid creator must **never buy Digi to film it**. Gift them access with an offer code instead.
   - Every creator dollar must be traceable to customers, so give each creator their own code.
6. **Campus programs are cheap and documented.**
   - BeReal paid student ambassadors **$30 per referral**, and each student who downloaded the app and sent the ambassador a review got **$50** (early 2022) [C, Brown Daily Herald, Feb 2022, via TechCrunch]. Paying for reviews would break today's FTC rule; copy the ambassador model, not the review payment. Its DAU reached a **peak of about 15M (Oct 2022)** [V, Wikipedia; one cited source says 20M]. The ~2.9M April 2022 starting point is not in Wikipedia today (unverified).
   - Today, the Home From College marketplace charges brands **$49/month** plus a 20% fee, and suggests paying students **$75–100 per video or $30 per hour** [V, H\FC pricing].
7. **Egyptian and MENA influencers can't help the contest.**
   - Digi launches US-only, so their audiences can't download it.
   - Cairo micro-influencers are modelled at **EGP 12,375–74,250 per post (~$231–1,383 at 53.68 EGP/USD, Nov 17, 2026)** [C, Elev8or, low]. That is not cheap for reach that can't convert.
   - Use Egypt for *production* (editing, slideshow making), not for reach.
8. **Snapchat Lenses are a free side door.** A Digi community Lens (flash + date stamp) costs $0. Snap's Lens Creator Rewards list **Egypt as an eligible country** [V, Lens Studio docs]. Sponsored Lenses are sales-led and far too expensive [C].
9. **Weddings and Greek life are real markets, but not December ones.**
   - The average 2025 US wedding: **$34,000, 117 guests, 13 vendors** [V, The Knot 2026 Real Weddings Study].
   - Bid Day and recruitment are mostly Aug–Sep and January [E, med].
   - For Dec 1–16, aim at **winter formals, date parties, Friendsgiving, office parties and NYE**.
10. **Recommended cash cap for the 30 days: about $450** (scenario B in section 8), all of it buying reusable real-person videos.
    - Rev-share payouts sit on top. They only happen when revenue does.
    - No boosting unless a post already beats 3× the account median.

**What to make because of it (E, med–high):** the *send* is the goal. Formats that get sent:

1. **"Tag/send the friend" carousels.** Slide 1: "send this to whoever's hosting NYE 📸". Slides 2–6: six Digi party strips. Last slide: what the app is and how to get it.
2. **Group-specific identity posts.**
   - "the friend who always has the digicam vs the friend who has Digi"
   - "your group chat after the Christmas party: 412 blurry photos, 0 usable. vs: [strip]"
3. **Useful saves.** "4 photo booth poses for a group of 4 (save before Friday)". These earn saves and sends.
4. **Before/after with the stamp.** iPhone night photo → the same photo with flash and an orange `'26 12 24` stamp. This is the format that earlier chapters showed drove WayShot and Boothie.
5. **Trial Reels for hooks.** Post each new hook as a Trial Reel to non-followers first, and promote to the main grid only the ones that beat the median (C feature; E tactic).

**Specs to use:**
- Carousels at 4:5 (1080×1350), up to 20 slides.
- Reels at 9:16, 7–15 s for the reveal format.

### 2.4 Sharing from Digi to an Instagram Story: what's possible

**Facts [V, [Meta docs](https://developers.facebook.com/docs/instagram-platform/sharing-to-stories), [2022 notice](https://developers.facebook.com/blog/post/2022/10/10/introducing-important-update-to-Instagram-sharing-to-stories/)]:**
- Since **January 2023** you "must provide a Facebook AppID to share content to Instagram Stories". Without it, users see an error.
- iOS opens `instagram-stories://share?source_application=<FB_APP_ID>`.
- iOS pasteboard keys:
  - `com.instagram.sharedSticker.backgroundImage`
  - `com.instagram.sharedSticker.stickerImage`
  - `…backgroundTopColor`
  - `…backgroundBottomColor`
- Size limits:
  - background image at least **720×1280** (9:16 recommended);
  - sticker image about 640×480 recommended;
  - background video H.264/H.265, **≤20 s**, up to 1080p, under 50 MB recommended;
  - default background colour #222222 if no colours are given.
- **There is no link or attribution field.** The App ID identifies the app to Meta, but it doesn't add a tappable link.

**Digi spec (E, med–high):**
1. **Two share modes:**
   - **"Story card":** the strip as a *sticker image* on Digi's pink top/bottom gradient, so the user can move and resize it.
   - **"Full photo":** the photo as a 1080×1920 background with the stamp burned in.
2. **Keep the top ~250 px and bottom ~340 px clear** of key content, because Instagram's UI covers them (C, common safe-zone guidance; check on a device).
3. **Put the brand inside the stamp**, for example `'26 12 31  digi.pink`. The stamp is the watermark, as chapter 11 recommends.
4. **Link step (test on a real iPhone):** when the user taps "Story", also put the share-page URL (`digi.pink/s/abc123`) on the clipboard. Then show a one-line toast before handing off: **"Link copied. Add the 🔗 sticker and paste to let friends make theirs."**
   - Instagram's own keys use the same pasteboard, so add the URL as a plain-text item in the *same* pasteboard write.
   - Then check the Link sticker can paste it. **Unverified (E, low)**: if it fails, drop the toast and rely on the stamp.
5. **Create the Meta developer app (free) this week** to get the App ID. It's an owner task: Meta developer dashboard → Create app. The App ID is public, not a secret, but keep the app secret out of the code.

### 2.5 Exact share-screen copy for Digi

## 3. Snapchat

### 3.1 Why Snapchat matters for a party camera (E, med)
- 56% of US 18–29s use it (V, Pew 2026). It's the default way to send photos *during* a night out.
- A party strip is the kind of thing that gets sent to a group chat that night.
- Snapchat is the **only one of the three platforms where Digi can attach a tappable link to the share itself** (§3.2). That makes it the best channel for share-to-install.

### 3.2 Creative Kit: share with a link [V]
From [Snap docs](https://developers.snap.com/snap-kit/creative-kit/overview):
- "Creative Kit allows users to share AR experiences (Lenses), stickers, images, videos, or captions."
- No SDK install needed (it's code-only).
- Media must be ≤300 MB; videos ≤5 min.
- Captions ≤250 characters.
- **One sticker only**: PNG/JPEG under 1 MB, or animated WebP/GIF on iOS.
- The iOS reference says a share can carry **an attachment URL, a caption or a sticker** [V, [iOS reference](https://developers.snap.com/reference/CreativeKit/ios/index.html)].
- **Review:** "Snap reviews all apps that seek to integrate with Snap Kit prior to enabling integration for public use… typically reviewed within **10–15 business days**."
  - You need a demo video of the integration, your own privacy-policy link, and app and integration descriptions [V, [review page](https://developers.snap.com/snap-kit/app-review/submission-and-review)].
- **Creative Kit Web** works from a web page (`data-share-url`, `snapchat:sticker` meta tag, sticker 400×400 transparent, under 1 MB). If no sticker is given, Snapchat 11.58+ makes one from `og:title`, `og:image` and the favicon. Snap's own example sticker reads "Swipe up to read" [V, [web docs](https://developers.snap.com/snap-kit/creative-kit/web)].
  - **digi.pink/s/ share pages can carry a Snapchat button too.**
- **URL attachments are gated** [V, [review page](https://developers.snap.com/snap-kit/app-review/submission-and-review), re-checked Nov 17]: "URL attachments are only allowed for select developers and for pre-approved use cases." Snap gives news articles, music, long-form video, donations, polls and RSVPs as examples, and "strictly prohibit[s]" uses such as "direct app downloads". So the link must point to the shared photo page (`digi.pink/s/<id>`), never to the App Store, and the review form must describe it in detail. A party-photo page is not on Snap's example list, so plan for a "no" (E, med).
- **Caveat:** a Snap developer-forum thread reports attachment URLs "not working for both Android & iOS" (undated) [C, [forum](https://community.snap.com/snapkit/discussion/comment/5147)]. Test before you rely on it.

**Digi spec (E, med):**
- Snap share = the photo or strip, plus attachment URL `digi.pink/s/<id>?src=snap` **if Snap approves URL attachments for Digi**, plus one small sticker: a pink pill that reads **"made with digi ↗"**. If the URL is refused, the sticker plus the `digi.pink` text inside the date stamp carry the brand, as on Instagram.
- Caption is pre-filled and editable: **"the strip from tonight 📸"**.
- **Timeline:** submit the Snap Kit integration as soon as the share screen exists in a TestFlight build, **target Nov 21**. Approval around Dec 5–12 (E). That's still before Christmas parties and NYE.

### 3.4 Spotlight and the public profile
- Spotlight is growing (+130% posters, +40% viewers YoY, Q3 2026) [V].
- **Monetization needs:**
  - **50K followers**;
  - 25+ posts a month;
  - posting on 10 of the last 28 days;
  - plus one of: 10M Snap views, 1M Spotlight views, or 12,000 hours of view time in 28 days [V, [Snap newsroom](https://newsroom.snap.com/snapchat-new-creator-monetization), Dec 16, 2024].
- **Digi doesn't need monetization, only reach.**
  - Cross-post every Reel to Spotlight. It's free and takes 1 minute.

### 5.4 Break-even math under contest rules (E, med; assumptions shown)

**Value of one payer inside the window:**
- Draft prices: yearly $39.99, weekly $6.99, Party Pack $5.99. Apple Small Business 15% commission.
- Mix: 45% yearly (after the trial), 35% weekly, 20% packs.
- Each kind of payer, net of Apple's commission:
  - yearly payer: **$33.99** net;
  - weekly payer: about **$11.65** net (1.96 payments in the window, per 05 §3.2);
  - pack buyer: **$5.09** net.
- Blend ≈ 0.45×33.99 + 0.35×11.65 + 0.20×5.09 ≈ **$20.4**.
- After refunds (~4%) and trial cancels already in the mix: **≈ $19 per payer** (E, med).
- AI cost isn't deducted in the contest.

**Funnel from one creator post (E, low–med):**
- Views → profile/link taps: 0.8–1.5%.
- Taps → installs: 35–50%.
- Installs → payers in the window: 4–6% (hard paywall after the first result; RevenueCat's hard-paywall D35 median is 10.7% [V, chapter 05]).
- Payers per 1,000 views ≈ 1,000 × 1% × 40% × 5% ≈ **0.2** (range 0.11–0.45).

| Deal | Cash out | Payers needed (÷$19) | Views needed at 0.2/1K (range) | Realistic? (E) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Micro creator flat post | $150 | 8 | **~40K** (18K–72K) | Coin flip. A 10–50K-follower account often gets fewer views than followers on a sponsored post |
| Instagram-paid average | $193 | 10 | ~50K (23K–92K) | Unlikely in the window |
| Billo UGC video posted on Digi's 4–5 own accounts | $99 | 5–6 | ~27K *across all reposts* (12K–49K) | **Good odds.** One video becomes 5+ posts plus hook re-cuts |
| H\FC student video ($85 + 20%) | $102 | 6 | ~27K | OK *if* rights let Digi repost it. Put that in the gig text |
| **Rev-share 30% on first payment** | $0 up front; ~$6 per payer | — | — | **Can't lose money** (payout < revenue) |
| Gifted access (offer code) only | $0 cash | — | — | Best, if the organisers say gifts aren't "paid marketing" (Q6 in 09) |

**Conclusion (E, med–high):**
- **Buy videos, not posts.**
- **Pay for reach only as a share of revenue.**
- Never pay a flat fee for reach after Dec 4. Exams start Dec 5 and nobody will be checking the results.

### 5.5 Deal structures that are profit-safe and rule-safe

**1. "Gift + rev-share" creator code (default).**
- The creator gets a gift code for Digi Plus (Apple offer code) plus **30% of the first payment** from users who enter their code.
- The users get **+5 bonus digis** when they enter the code in the app ("Got a creator code?").
- That gives attribution (rules §6: "show which customers… that spend brought in") and a reason to type it.
- Pay out after Apple's refund window, around Jan 5. **Log every payout as marketing spend** with the customer list.
- **Why this is the default:** as computed above, it's the only deal that can't lose money.

**2. Billo / H\FC UGC (cash, capped).**
- Content only. You own it, and you post it on Digi accounts with the AI label where relevant.
- **Brief rule: the creator films with a gifted Digi account.** Write "we'll send you a free access code, please don't purchase" into the brief. This protects the "anyone you pay buy it" rule.

**3. Spark / Partnership Ads boost (cash, only on winners).**
- Only when a creator post or own post beats **3× the account's median views within 48 h**.
- Cap **$25/day for 3 days**. Kill if cost per install is over $2.50 by day 2 (E).

**4. Ambassadors (campus).**
- Paid in digis and Plus access, plus a per-paying-referral bonus of $5 (rev-share logic: only pays out on revenue).
- No per-download cash. BeReal's 2022 model paid $50 per download plus review [C]. **Paying for reviews breaks Apple's rules** (Guideline 3.2.2 bans "manipulat[ing]… ratings or reviews") [C, from memory]. Don't copy that part.

**What not to do:**
- Pay for "reviews".
- Let creators pose as random customers ("I just found this app").
- Ask anyone to "support my entry".
- Use AI people posing as real users.

### 5.7 Disclosure rules (FTC) [V]
From [FTC "Disclosures 101"](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/resources/disclosures-101-social-media-influencers):
- A material connection includes "the brand paying you or giving you **free or discounted products or services**". **Gifted Digi Plus counts.**
- On Snapchat and Instagram Stories, "**superimpose the disclosure over the picture**".
- "Don't assume that a platform's disclosure tool is good enough." Use the tool *and* the words "ad" or "sponsored".
- Avoid "sp", "spon" and "collab", and "ambassador" on its own.
- The endorser must actually use the product. 16 CFR 255.1(c) requires a "bona fide user" when an ad says the endorser uses it [C, from memory; eCFR blocked automated reading today].
- So creators *must* use Digi before posting. Gifting the code early is a rule requirement, not a nice-to-have.
